# IT HelpDesk Frequently Asked Questions

## Password and Account Access

### How do I reset my password?
1. Visit the self-service portal at https://portal.company.com
2. Click on "Forgot Password"
3. Enter your employee ID and registered email address
4. Check your email for a verification code
5. Enter the verification code on the portal
6. Create a new password following the requirements:
   - Minimum 12 characters
   - At least one uppercase letter
   - At least one lowercase letter
   - At least one number
   - At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
   - Cannot reuse your last 5 passwords

**Note**: If you don't receive the verification code within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or contact the IT HelpDesk.

### My account is locked. What should I do?
Your account locks automatically after 5 failed login attempts for security reasons.

**To unlock your account:**
1. Wait 30 minutes for automatic unlock, OR
2. Visit the self-service portal at https://portal.company.com and use the "Unlock Account" option, OR
3. Contact the IT HelpDesk at ext. 5555 for immediate assistance

### How do I change my password?
You can change your password at any time:
1. Press Ctrl+Alt+Delete on Windows or Cmd+Option+Escape on Mac
2. Select "Change Password"
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter and confirm your new password
5. Click "OK"

Alternatively, change it through the self-service portal at https://portal.company.com.

### How often do I need to change my password?
Passwords must be changed every 90 days for security compliance. You'll receive reminders starting 14 days before expiration.

## VPN and Remote Access

### How do I connect to the VPN?
**First-time setup:**
1. Ensure you have VPN client software installed (available in Software Center)
2. Open the VPN client
3. Enter the server address: vpn.company.com
4. Use your regular work username and password
5. Click "Connect"

**Troubleshooting VPN connection issues:**
- Verify your internet connection is working
- Check if your password has expired
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting
- Restart the VPN client
- Restart your computer
- Contact IT HelpDesk if issues persist

### What should I do if VPN keeps disconnecting?
Common solutions:
1. Update your VPN client to the latest version
2. Check your internet connection stability
3. Disable battery optimization for the VPN app (mobile)
4. Try a different network (e.g., switch from WiFi to mobile data)
5. Contact IT HelpDesk if disconnections continue

### Can I use the VPN from any location?
Yes, you can connect from anywhere with an internet connection. However, some countries may have restrictions. Contact IT HelpDesk before traveling internationally for work.

## Printers and Printing

### How do I add a network printer?
**On Windows:**
1. Click Start > Settings > Devices > Printers & scanners
2. Click "Add a printer or scanner"
3. Select your printer from the list
4. If not listed, click "The printer that I want isn't listed"
5. Select "Add a printer using a TCP/IP address or hostname"
6. Contact IT HelpDesk for the printer's IP address

**On Mac:**
1. Click Apple menu > System Preferences > Printers & Scanners
2. Click the "+" button
3. Select your printer from the list
4. Click "Add"

### How do I print from my mobile device?
Most office printers support mobile printing:

**For iOS:**
Use AirPrint (if supported) or download the "Company Print" app from the App Store

**For Android:**
Download the "Company Print" app from Google Play Store

Contact IT HelpDesk for your printer's mobile printing setup code.

### My print job is stuck in the queue. What should I do?
1. Open the printer queue on your computer
2. Right-click on the stuck job and select "Cancel"
3. Wait a few moments
4. Try printing again

If the issue persists:
1. Restart your computer
2. Check if the printer is online and has paper/ink
3. Contact IT HelpDesk

## Hardware Issues

### My laptop/desktop is running very slow. How can I fix it?
Common solutions:
1. **Restart your computer** - This resolves many performance issues
2. **Close unnecessary applications** - Check Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc)
3. **Clear browser cache and cookies**
4. **Check for Windows updates** - Settings > Update & Security
5. **Run disk cleanup** - Search for "Disk Cleanup" in Start menu
6. **Check available disk space** - You need at least 10% free space

If slowness continues, create a ServiceNow ticket for IT to investigate.

### My computer won't turn on. What should I do?
**For laptops:**
1. Connect the power adapter
2. Look for power indicator lights
3. Press and hold the power button for 10 seconds, then try again
4. Try a different power outlet

**For desktops:**
1. Check all power cables are securely connected
2. Check if the monitor is powered on separately
3. Listen for any beeps or fan noise
4. Try a different power outlet

If still not working, create a high-priority ServiceNow ticket immediately.

### How do I request a hardware replacement or upgrade?
1. Create a ServiceNow ticket describing the issue or need
2. Include:
   - Current hardware details
   - Reason for replacement/upgrade
   - Business justification
   - Manager approval (if required)

**Processing time**: Hardware requests typically take 3-5 business days depending on availability.

### Can I use my personal laptop for work?
Personal devices may be permitted for specific use cases under the BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) policy:
1. Contact IT HelpDesk to verify eligibility
2. Complete the BYOD enrollment process
3. Install required security software
4. Agree to security and data policies

**Note**: Not all systems and data are accessible from personal devices.

## Network and Connectivity

### I can't connect to WiFi. What should I do?
1. **Check if WiFi is enabled** on your device
2. **Forget and reconnect** to the network:
   - Windows: Network settings > Manage known networks > Forget
   - Mac: System Preferences > Network > WiFi > Advanced > Remove network
3. **Restart your device**
4. **Move closer to an access point**
5. **Check if others can connect** - may be a network issue

**Corporate WiFi credentials:**
- Network: CorpWiFi
- Username: Your work email
- Password: Your regular work password

### What's the difference between CorpWiFi and CorpGuest?
- **CorpWiFi**: For employees and company devices only. Requires corporate credentials. Provides access to internal resources.
- **CorpGuest**: For visitors and guest devices. No credentials required. Provides internet access only, no internal resources.

### How do I report a network outage?
1. Verify it's not just your device (ask colleagues)
2. Create a ServiceNow ticket marking it as "Critical" priority
3. Call IT HelpDesk at ext. 5555 for immediate assistance
4. Provide location details (building, floor, area)

## File Storage and Sharing

### How do I access my files remotely?
All work files should be stored in one of these locations, accessible anywhere:
1. **OneDrive for Business** - Personal work files
2. **SharePoint** - Team and department files
3. **Shared Drives** - Accessible via VPN

**Never store work files only on your local computer** - they won't be backed up.

### How do I share files with colleagues?
**For internal sharing:**
1. Save the file to OneDrive or SharePoint
2. Right-click the file > Share
3. Enter colleague's email address
4. Set permissions (view or edit)
5. Add optional message
6. Click "Send"

**For external sharing:**
Follow company data security policies. Contact IT HelpDesk for guidance on sharing sensitive information with external parties.

### What's the difference between OneDrive and SharePoint?
- **OneDrive**: Your personal workspace for work files. Only you have access unless you share.
- **SharePoint**: Team sites for collaborative work. Multiple team members have access by default.

**Best practice**: Use OneDrive for personal work files, SharePoint for team collaboration.

### How much storage space do I have?
- **OneDrive for Business**: 1 TB per user
- **Email (Outlook)**: 50 GB mailbox, 25 MB attachment limit
- **SharePoint team sites**: Varies by site, typically several TB per site

Contact IT HelpDesk if you need additional storage.

## Security and Compliance

### What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?
**Signs of phishing emails:**
- Urgent action required
- Suspicious sender address
- Requests for passwords or personal information
- Unexpected attachments or links
- Spelling and grammar errors

**If you receive a suspicious email:**
1. **Do NOT click any links or attachments**
2. **Do NOT reply or provide information**
3. Report it using the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook
4. Delete the email
5. Contact IT Security if you already clicked anything

### How do I report a security incident?
1. **Immediately** contact IT Security at ext. 5599 or security@company.com
2. Do not attempt to fix it yourself
3. Disconnect from the network if you suspect a compromise
4. Preserve evidence - don't delete anything
5. Create a high-priority ServiceNow ticket

**Examples of security incidents:**
- Lost or stolen devices
- Suspected malware or virus
- Unauthorized access to accounts
- Data breach or leak
- Phishing attacks

### What are the password requirements?
**All passwords must:**
- Be at least 12 characters long
- Include uppercase letters (A-Z)
- Include lowercase letters (a-z)
- Include numbers (0-9)
- Include special characters (!@#$%^&*)
- Not contain your username or email
- Not reuse your last 5 passwords
- Be changed every 90 days

### Can I use the same password for multiple accounts?
**No.** Each system should have a unique password. Use a password manager if needed (contact IT HelpDesk for approved options).

### Is multi-factor authentication (MFA) required?
Yes, MFA is required for:
- VPN access
- Email access from external devices
- All cloud applications
- Remote desktop connections

**Setting up MFA:**
1. Visit https://aka.ms/mfasetup
2. Sign in with your work credentials
3. Follow the setup wizard
4. Choose your verification method (app, SMS, or phone call)
5. Complete the setup and save backup codes

## Common Software Issues

### Outlook is not syncing. What should I do?
1. Check your internet connection
2. Click "Send/Receive All Folders" in Outlook
3. Restart Outlook
4. Restart your computer
5. Check if you're working in "Offline Mode" - click "Send/Receive" tab and ensure "Work Offline" is not selected

If still not syncing, create a ServiceNow ticket.

### I'm getting "unlicensed product" error in Microsoft Office. How do I fix it?
1. Close all Office applications
2. Open any Office app (e.g., Word)
3. Sign out: File > Account > Sign out
4. Sign back in with your work email
5. Activate: File > Account > Activate Office

If the issue persists, create a ServiceNow ticket.

### My browser is slow or crashing frequently
**Quick fixes:**
1. Clear cache and cookies
2. Disable unnecessary extensions
3. Update to the latest browser version
4. Try a different browser
5. Restart your computer

**Approved browsers:**
- Microsoft Edge (recommended)
- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox

**Note**: Internet Explorer is no longer supported.

### How do I clear my browser cache?
**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
2. Select time range (e.g., "All time")
3. Check "Cached images and files"
4. Click "Clear now"

**Chrome:**
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
2. Select time range
3. Check "Cached images and files"
4. Click "Clear data"

**Firefox:**
1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete
2. Select time range
3. Check "Cache"
4. Click "Clear Now"

### What information should I include when creating a ServiceNow ticket?
To help us resolve your issue quickly, include:
1. **Detailed description** of the problem
2. **When did it start?** (date/time)
3. **Error messages** (exact text or screenshots)
4. **Steps to reproduce** the issue
5. **What you've already tried** to fix it
6. **Impact on your work** (urgency)
7. **Device information** (computer name, model)
8. **Location** (if hardware issue)

### How long will it take to resolve my issue?
**Response times by priority:**
- **Critical**: 1 hour (system down, security incident)
- **High**: 4 hours (major functionality impaired)
- **Medium**: 1 business day (minor functionality impaired)
- **Low**: 3 business days (cosmetic issues, questions)

**Resolution times vary** based on issue complexity and may require escalation to specialized teams.
